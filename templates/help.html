How can we help?

Guides for common pharmacy workflows.

Create a bill

Open Billing, search and add medicines, optionally select a patient, choose a payment method, review safety prompts, then complete the invoice.

Interaction checker

The local screening engine checks ingredients selected together against its installed reference entries. Treat findings as prompts for pharmacist review; the demo dataset is not comprehensive clinical decision support.

Inventory guide

Use Inventory to review batch expiry and stock status. Adjust stock with the controls and use CSV/PDF reports to export a snapshot.

Patient guide

Add patient demographics in Patients. Billing history links purchases to the selected patient.

Reports guide

Open Reports center and choose CSV for spreadsheet use or PDF for a printable snapshot.

Troubleshooting

If a page fails to load, refresh and sign in again. Keep a strong SECRET_KEY configured on hosted deployments. Demo SQLite data is not persistent on ephemeral hosting.

Accessibility

Open Settings to enable large text, high contrast, compact mode, reduced motion, dark theme, and review keyboard shortcuts. Preferences are stored in this browser.

FAQ · Does screening replace clinical review?

No. The bundled interaction records are a demo reference; use an authoritative current source and qualified judgment.
