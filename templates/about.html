Our purpose

About MediShield

A calmer workspace for everyday community pharmacy operations.

Mission

Give pharmacy teams a clear place to manage inventory, patient records, billing, and review prompts so daily workflows are easier to follow.

Vision

Support safer, more transparent pharmacy operations through readable tools that help teams review their work.

The problem we address

Disconnected registers and stock lists make routine work harder to review. MediShield brings those operational tasks into one practical workspace.

Core features

Medicine catalog, stock alerts, patient records, billing, invoice PDFs, reports, and interaction screening.

Technology stack

Flask · SQLAlchemy · SQLite · Chart.js · ReportLab

MediShield v1.0

Developer: MediShield project team

Application architecture

Flask routes serve authenticated pages and JSON APIs; SQLAlchemy persists pharmacy records in SQLite; templates and static assets render the responsive interface; ReportLab creates downloadable PDFs.
